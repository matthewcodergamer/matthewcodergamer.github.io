Editorial policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to the articles under Server guides. It does not turn the browser game into a newsroom. It exists so you can see how a hosting recommendation got onto the page.

Original work

Guides are written for this site. We do not scrape other hosts’ blogs and rewrite them. Commands that come from a vendor — Paper’s flag list, Adoptium’s repository line — are quoted as commands and linked. The explanation around them is ours.

How a host gets named

A company is named so you can recognize a category: shared game panel, KVM VPS, or dedicated machine. Naming is not a ranking we were paid for. We do not use affiliate links in these guides. If a link is ever an affiliate link, the article will say so in the first screen, and the recommendation will still have to survive without the commission. “We tested this” is reserved for steps we actually ran. Performance ranges are labeled as planning figures when they are not a measurement from our own world.

Updates

Each guide shows a “last updated” month. We revise when Paper or Java changes a flag, a config path, or a supported version, and when a price band in the article has gone stale enough to mislead. Small wording fixes do not always change the month. A change to commands does.

Corrections

Send corrections through the contact page with the URL and the source you are comparing against, preferably Paper’s documentation. We would rather fix a command than defend it.

Advertising and labels

Google AdSense may place ads on these pages. An ad is not an endorsement, and an advertiser does not review the guides before they are published. If an ad sits next to a sentence about a host, that proximity is the ad network’s placement, not a sponsorship.

Who it is for

The server guides are written for readers 16 and older who are choosing infrastructure. They are not directed at young children. The browser game is a separate feature and is described in the privacy policy.
