Privacy policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you use Minecraft Web at matthewcodergamer.github.io (the “site”), including the browser game at / and the articles under /guides/. The publisher is Matthew Coder, the maintainer of that site.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your worlds, settings, enabled mods and resource packs, and any purchase records it needs in your browser’s local storage on your own device. This data is not sent to us as a copy of your world. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. Clearing it can delete your worlds. The server guides do not ask you to create an account and do not store a profile.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices and, in Europe, youronlinechoices.com. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. If you refuse personalized ads, you may still see non-personalized ads, which do not use advertising cookies in the same way.

Purchases

Optional in-game items (such as mods, resource packs, shaders, and “Remove Ads”) are sold through Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe’s secure checkout page and are never sent to or stored by us. Stripe shares with us the information needed to confirm and restore your purchase: your email address, what you bought, the amount, and the payment status. We use it only to deliver and restore your purchases and to handle refunds or support requests.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not run a separate analytics suite that builds advertising profiles of readers of the guides.

Children

The server guides are written for readers 16 and older and are not directed at children under 13. The site as a whole is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Purchases require a payment method and should be made by, or with the permission of, a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete what we actually hold.

Your choices

You can refuse non-essential cookies in the consent message where it is shown, use the Google and industry opt-out links above, and clear this site’s storage in your browser. If you are in a region that gives you a right to access or delete personal information a business holds, email is not how this site is run: open a request through the contact page and say which right you are using. Purchases are the main case where a name or email reaches us, and that record lives with Stripe as well as with us.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your purchases: the contact page, or an issue at github.com/matthewcodergamer/MinecraftWeb/issues. Please do not include a card number.
