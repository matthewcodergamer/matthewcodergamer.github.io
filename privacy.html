Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit matthewcodergamer.github.io (the “Website”), play the Minecraft Web browser game, or read the Website’s guides. The Website is published and maintained by Matthew Coder (“we”, “us” or “our”).

In short: Game-world information is stored in your own browser on your device. The Website uses Google advertising technology. GitHub hosts the Website and may process technical request information. We do not operate a Website account system or ask visitors to submit personal details to play.

1. Information stored by the game on your device

The browser game uses browser storage to keep game data available between visits. In particular, world metadata is stored in your browser’s local storage and imported world files are stored in IndexedDB. This information is stored locally by your browser for this Website; it is not uploaded to us as a cloud world save.

The Website may also use browser local storage for small site-functionality markers. Browser storage is associated with the browser and device you use. It may be removed when you clear site data, use private browsing, change browsers or devices, or when your browser evicts stored data. Clearing it may permanently remove locally stored worlds or imported files. Export or back up anything important before clearing browser data.

You can manage or delete this information through your browser’s site-data and storage settings. The exact controls differ by browser and device.

2. Information processed when you visit

The Website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When a browser requests a page or file, GitHub and the infrastructure needed to deliver the Website may process technical information such as an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or device information, and security or diagnostic information. We do not control GitHub’s infrastructure logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We do not currently operate a Website registration system, visitor profile database, or first-party advertising analytics account on these pages. If you choose to contact the project by opening a GitHub issue, the information you submit is handled by GitHub and may be publicly visible. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, private documents, or other sensitive information in a public issue.

The Website displays or may request advertising provided by Google, including Google AdSense and related Google advertising technology. The publisher account associated with the Website is ca-pub-5025566183652379. Google and other advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, local storage, or similar identifiers to deliver, measure, limit the frequency of, and report on advertisements. These technologies may allow a provider to recognise your browser on this or other websites.

Depending on your location, the consent choices available, and the applicable ad request, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Personalised advertising can use information about your visits to this and other websites. Non-personalised advertising may still use cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting, and combating fraud. The exact technologies and data processed are determined by Google and the relevant advertising partners.

Google explains its data practices here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can review or change Google advertising settings at My Ad Center or Google Ads Settings. You can also visit aboutads.info for information about industry opt-outs. These controls do not disable every form of advertising or data processing.

The Website also loads Google Publisher Tag and, on selected pages, Google Reader Revenue Manager scripts. These services may process information as described in Google’s own privacy documentation when their scripts are used. Their presence does not mean that every visitor will necessarily receive an advertisement or a prompt.

For information about Google advertising cookies and publisher disclosures, see Google AdSense: Required content and Google Publisher Policies: Privacy disclosures.

Where required, advertising consent choices should be presented through the Website’s configured consent-management mechanism before the relevant optional advertising processing takes place. Google requires publishers serving personalised ads in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF).

This policy describes the Website’s practices; it is not itself a consent banner and does not record or transmit a consent choice. Your choices must be made using the consent message or privacy controls actually presented on the Website. 6. How long information is kept

Game data remains in your browser storage until you delete it, the browser removes it, or the game changes how it stores data. Technical logs and information processed by GitHub, Google, or other providers are retained according to those providers’ own policies and settings. If you submit a GitHub issue, its retention and visibility are governed by GitHub and the repository’s settings.

7. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about personal data, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have rights to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exceptions.

For data held in your browser, you can use your browser’s site-data controls. For data held by Google or GitHub, you may need to contact the relevant provider directly using its privacy tools. For a question about information the Website publisher may hold, use the Contact page. The project’s public GitHub issue tracker is not a private channel, so do not post personal data there; ask for a suitable private contact route instead.

8. Children’s privacy

The Website is not designed to request personal information from children. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information through a Website account or registration form. The Website’s guides may contain technical material intended for older readers. Parents or guardians should supervise children’s use of online services and advertising. If you believe personal information about a child has been submitted to a public project issue, contact GitHub about the public content and contact the project through the Contact page.

9. Security

No website, browser, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your browser and device updated, do not share private account credentials, and do not treat locally stored worlds as backed-up or encrypted cloud data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Website, its services, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top identifies the latest revision. The current version will be published at this URL.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Contact page. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.
