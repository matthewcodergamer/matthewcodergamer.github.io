Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use Minecraft Web at matthewcodergamer.github.io (the "site"), including the game at /MinecraftWeb/.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your worlds, settings, enabled mods and resource packs, and any purchases in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

The site may show ads served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info. To learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Purchases

Optional in-game items (such as mods, resource packs, shaders and "Remove Ads") are sold through Stripe. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout page and are never sent to or stored by us. Stripe shares with us the information needed to confirm and restore your purchase: your email address, what you bought, the amount and the payment status. We use it only to deliver and restore your purchases and to handle refunds or support requests.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you load the site; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Purchases require a payment method and should be made by, or with the permission of, a parent or guardian.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your purchases: open an issue at github.com/matthewcodergamer/MinecraftWeb/issues.
